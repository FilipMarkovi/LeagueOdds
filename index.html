How the app works

  • Connects to your live match: the app reads current game state from your running LoL client.
  • Builds a live snapshot: compares both teams using gold, levels, towers, dragons, barons, and grubs.
  • Predicts win chance: shows updated Blue vs Red odds at each 5-minute checkpoint.
  • Runs in the background: quick readouts while you play, with no manual data entry.
